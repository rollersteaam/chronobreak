Principle Emotion
Joyful - the activity is fun, rewarding, exciting. Doing it feels good in some particular way, like a game, where I succeeded in a difficult situation, after some sort of struggle, or got to feel cool roleplaying in a certain situation. Emotion is a natural challenge, and building features that work well and look good is naturally joyful. It’s difficult but incredibly empowering.
Meaningful - the activity has a purpose and results in the creation, building, or advancement of something or someone to a goal, and genuinely helps people's lives. It helps me achieve or make significant progress towards one of my major life goals of something I want to do before I die. Emotion, if it works, will help cure depression and anxiety for good by empowering people to act on their work and dreams with the power of music. It’s everything I want the future to be. People inspired to live. Engaged, every day. It could be responsible for billions of economic growth, but above that, it could help millions get out of their trapped lives and enter the limelight, a new world of hope brought by other humans - artists, not AI. If we can even just help one person believe in themselves and feel powerful most days, that's an unbelievable result and beats most anything I could have ever worked on.
Powerful - the activity led to me completing something that was incredibly difficult and special in some way, that distinguishes me over other people, and changed lives or caused a major result or difference in some way. It helped me greatly advance a skill in something that I really wanted to improve at. Launching Emotion as a startup would mean I followed through. It would train my ability in AI, software, and entrepreneurship, making me much better for any future works I will do in my life. It will look great as an achievement. It would be a mark of honour for me. Not an old story of something I’m working on, but something I worked on. And perhaps, something that was successful, among the crowds of companies and projects that are not. I chose, of my own will, to make this happen. To push past all the despair, roughness, and fear, to give birth to something, just because I said fuck it, let’s go. That’s one of the most respectable things anyone can do, no matter how messy the road you take.
Secure - the activity took me closer to establishing a more financially and emotionally stable life at some point in time, or even immediately, and fulfilled or helped one of my more basic needs. If Emotion works as a business idea, it could bring in millions in revenue a year with relative ease. My future would literally be set for life. And if it doesn’t work, the IP we learn could be brought to Spotify, making a massive jump in salary, especially due to the scale of the work. While it won’t bring immediate financial safety, it has the possibility to remove it as a concern for good. With that will come emotional stability, among all the other things we’re doing to make our lives feel fuller and calmer.
Clear - the next step for where I spend my energy is clear and will lead to the results I want from the energy spent. Doing any hard work that actually achieves the goal we need is valuable. It doesn’t matter if it’s not urgent, it is valuable. Creating and launching this beta is the clear next step. It will bring you one step closer to being a real business and you can present this technology in every chat you have. After you make it, you can cold message Spotify employees to get an audience.
Praised - people look more favourably on you for making progress or achieving certain results in the activity. The people who are waiting for this, that you’ve iterated it with, will be elated that they can function to a level they didn’t think possible. The potentially 1000s of people you haven’t even met yet will be unbelievably grateful after they find it, because you made something that helps them succeed in life. You took the brave sacrifice to do that. If you want praise, or to be respected or favoured, there’s no real greater calling than building something of your own will and making it work just to help other people, and sacrificing your free time to do it.
